## 1. What changed, in plain words

### Trial mode

- A database built by the demo seed is in **trial mode**. It holds the demo company: invented people, history and
  records, and the one-tap demo sign-ins on the sign-in page.
- While in trial mode every administrator sees **"Trial mode: demo sign-ins are on"** under the top bar on every page
  (or "Trial mode: the demo company is still here" when the one-tap sign-ins are off), with **How to go live** →
  Settings → Trial mode. Needs attention starts with the same item (danger) for administrators, above every other
  item, old failed transfers included: "Trial mode: demo sign-ins are on. Go live before real pay or bank details go
  in. Run npm run golive on the computer that runs Linear Hub."
- During the trial anyone on the office Wi-Fi can open the one-tap demo accounts, so a demo account (and every person
  of the demo company) may only play with the demo: its own account, the demo company's people and records, and new
  records it makes (an announcement, a candidate, an event, an expense…). It can never:
  - change a person of the office's own: their details, role, status, employee ID, sign-in, client IDs, gauges,
    learning sign-offs or bookings;
  - touch anyone's pay but the demo company's: pay adjustments and bonuses, paying a pay run, payment runs (make,
    mark paid, cancel), invoices of real people;
  - see or change a real person's bank details, or download a payment file;
  - change office-wide settings: sign-in security, pay settings and rates, a campaign's pay per sale, payment
    connections (add, test, change, remove, go live), company details on invoices, statement mail and templates, the
    interview questions, video settings;
  - approve a payment, create or promote an administrator, reset anyone's sign-in, or give anyone a setup code;
  - change a record the office made in the app (an event, a candidate, a statement, a video…).
  It gets 403 "Demo accounts cannot … Sign in with your own administrator account, or go live first (npm run
  golive)." Every write the app has is on one list (`server/src/lib/demoWrites.ts`); a new write nobody has put on
  the list is refused for demo accounts until someone decides.
- Demo sessions follow the normal session limit (14 hours by default, Settings → Security), not 14 days.
- **The one-tap demo sign-ins are off by default on a server that can be reached from the internet**: one with
  built-in HTTPS (`LM_TLS_CERT_FILE`) or behind a reverse proxy (`TRUST_PROXY`). `LM_DEMO_ACCOUNTS=1` shows them
  anyway; `LM_DEMO_ACCOUNTS=0` turns them off everywhere. On the office Mac (plain http on the Wi-Fi) they stay on
  until going live. The start-up lines say when trial mode is on but the sign-ins are off for this reason.

### Going live: `npm run golive`

Run on the computer that runs Linear Hub, with Linear Hub stopped:

1. It refuses while the database is open (Linear Hub running on any port, or as a background service) and says how
   to stop it (`npm run stop`, or `npm run service:uninstall` for the background service).
2. It shows what goes and what stays, and asks for the first owner's name and email when no administrator of the
   office's own would be left.
3. It asks you to type **GO LIVE** (scripts: `--confirm "GO LIVE"`).
4. It backs the database up and checks the backup (integrity check, same number of people) before changing anything.
5. It removes the demo company: its people (including the one-tap demo accounts, even if renamed: anyone on the
   network could open them) and their history, and the records the demo seed made (teams, campaigns, events,
   expenses, candidates, announcements, statements, learning videos), **except** a demo record that:
   - someone of the office's own made or changed in the app (the audit trail shows it);
   - names one of the office's people (for example a team they are in or lead);
   - something that stays points at (read from the database's own links, plus a few columns without one): a
     campaign with a real person's client ID, pay rate, sales, statement or client post; the team and campaign of an
     event the office's people logged on; and so on, until nothing more changes.
   A view or a read never keeps a record. An announcement a demo account wrote after the seed passes to the owner.
6. It keeps the settings (pay plans, payment connections, security and company settings), the office's own people
   and their history, every record made in the app since the demo was set up, and the demo records above. **Statement
   PDFs are never deleted** (the backup golive makes holds the database, not the statements folder).
7. Before asking, the plan lists, by name: the demo accounts used during the trial (changes, sign-ins, last use) —
   they go too, so whoever used one adds their own account; the demo records it keeps and why ("campaign Breast
   Cancer Now (BCN): client ID of …"); and the demo records only demo accounts changed (they go).
8. Trial mode is off for good: no one-tap sign-ins, no demo start-up steps, the server never seeds it again.
9. When no administrator of the office's own is left it makes the first **owner** (employee ID LM-0001 when free) and
   prints their one-time setup code. With `--owner-email …` it makes that owner even when a colleague stays an
   administrator (the person going live used a demo account). Every person of the office's own who has not finished
   setting up is listed ("give each a setup code").
10. Running it again changes nothing ("Linear Hub is already live").

On an upgraded **1.4 trial database** the first start of 1.5 works out which records the demo seed made from the
evidence (below the first record the audit trail shows the app made, and below the first statement that arrived
after the seed), so a trial week already spent on 1.4 is kept. Checked on a 1.4.0 database with a scripted trial week:
the candidate, announcement, expense and a real rep's client ID on a demo campaign all stayed.

**A wrong `DB_PATH`:** the server no longer makes a new demo company where the backups folder shows a database was;
it stops and says to check `DB_PATH`, or `npm run restore -- <newest>`, or `npm run seed` to start a demo on purpose.

Flags for scripts: `--confirm "GO LIVE"`, `--owner-email`, `--owner-first-name`, `--owner-last-name`. Reads `DB_PATH`,
`PORT`, `LM_BACKUP_DIR` and `LM_APP_SECRET_FILE` like `npm start`. Exit 0 live (now or already), 1 refused or
cancelled with nothing changed. An audit row records it ("Went live from the server command: removed the demo
company (N people, M records); made … the owner").

### One-time setup codes

- Every new account (Team → Add person, the administrators' form, a hire in Recruitment, a client invite, the owner
  `npm run golive` makes) and every administrator reset of someone's two-step verification or PIN gets a **one-time
  setup code**: 12 letters and digits in three groups (`ABCD-EFGH-JKMN`, no I, L, O, 0 or 1), shown once, valid
  7 days, single use. Only a SHA-256 hash is stored; a new code replaces any unused one.
- An account that has not finished setting up — no two-step verification yet, or an administrator reset its PIN —
  can only sign in with its setup code. Knowing someone's email and employee ID is no longer enough to start their
  account and choose its PIN and authenticator. One-tap demo accounts (trial mode) are exempt.
- After an administrator resets someone's PIN (two-step stays on), the code is used up only once their two-step code
  is right too: a sign-in abandoned at the two-step step leaves the code still needed, so nobody holding only their
  phone can get in and choose the PIN.
- The sign-in page asks for the code after the email and employee ID (and the PIN, if any): **Type your setup code**.
  A wrong, used or expired code gets one sentence: "That setup code is not right, or it has been used or has expired.
  Ask an administrator for a new one." Missing and wrong codes count against the sign-in limit.
- **Sign-in details** (after adding someone or a hire) show the setup code with the employee ID, and the instructions
  to copy include it. Team → the person → **Edit profile** → **Sign-in setup** → **Give a setup code** makes a new one.
  After **Reset two-step verification** or **Reset PIN** the dialog shows the code to hand over. Client accounts: the
  invite shows the access code and the setup code; **Reset PIN** / **Reset two-step** on the campaign page show the
  new code.
- On the server: `npm run setup-code -- <email>` prints a new code for anyone who has not finished setting up (for
  example the only administrator, or an account from 1.4). `npm run 2fa:reset` and `npm run pin:reset` now print the
  setup code the person needs next.

### The admin's own code for sensitive actions (step-up)

Creating an administrator, making someone an administrator, **stopping someone being an administrator, setting an
administrator as left**, changing someone's sign-in email (staff and client accounts) **or employee ID**, resetting
someone's two-step verification or PIN (staff and client accounts) and giving a setup code need the administrator's
**fresh 6-digit code** from their authenticator app (each code works once; wrong codes count
towards the same limit as at sign-in). An administrator without two-step verification is told to set it up first.
All of these are in the audit trail. Team → Edit profile shows the code box as soon as one of these changes is made
in the form, with the reason ("Changing someone's employee ID needs your code."). One code covers a change that is
sensitive twice over.

### Sessions

The sessions table keeps only a SHA-256 of each session token. Existing sessions are converted in place on the first
start of 1.5: nobody is signed out.

### Secure connections and built-in HTTPS

- Money and secret actions are refused (403 "For safety this only works over a secure connection. Open Linear Hub on
  the office computer itself, or use its https:// address.") over plain http from another device: entering or
  changing bank details (own or someone's, and confirming them), approving a payment run, downloading a payment file,
  adding a payment connection or changing one, giving a setup code, resetting two-step or a PIN (staff and client
  accounts), and making new recovery codes. The office computer itself (loopback) and https are fine.
- Over plain http from another device a new account is still made, but its setup code is not shown ("The setup code
  is not shown over an insecure connection. An administrator gives them one on the office computer or over https:
  Team → their name → Edit profile → Give a setup code."). Turning two-step on works, but the recovery codes are not
  shown (none are kept): make new ones later in Settings → Security over a secure connection.
- **Built-in HTTPS**: set `LM_TLS_CERT_FILE` and `LM_TLS_KEY_FILE` (PEM certificate and private key) and the server
  answers HTTPS on `PORT` (no plain-http port, no redirect). Cookies are then `Secure` and every answer carries
  `Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=15552000; includeSubDomains`. One without the other, or a file that cannot be
  read, stops the start with a sentence before the database is opened. The start-up lines, the "already running"
  check and `npm run share` use `https://`.
- Behind Caddy (DEPLOY route A) nothing changes: Caddy terminates TLS; requests reach Linear Hub on loopback.
  **With `TRUST_PROXY` set, `HOST` must be `127.0.0.1`**: otherwise anyone who reaches the port directly can send
  forged forwarded headers and look like https. The server now warns at start-up when `TRUST_PROXY` is set and
  `HOST` is not loopback.
- The Mac on Wi-Fi trial stays plain http: phones can use everything except the actions above, which work on the
  Mac itself. Someone who turns two-step on from their phone over the Wi-Fi gets no recovery codes; they make them in
  Settings → Security on the Mac (or over https after going live).

  (`morgan.blake@linearhub.example`, LM-0001) and every demo email uses the invented domain `linearhub.example`.
- The blank starter team (one-tap accounts during the trial) comes from `server/data/starter-team.json` next to the

- On a phone, a Needs-attention pop-up no longer sits over a dialog's buttons: opening any dialog puts those pop-ups

### `npm run seed` guards

`npm run seed` deletes the database and builds the demo again, so it now refuses:

- a database that has gone live (or never was the demo): "This database has gone live … nothing was changed";
- a trial database with people of the office's own (anyone but the demo company and the starter team the seed makes
  again), naming them;
- while Linear Hub has the database open (any port, or the background service);
- and asks you to type **START AGAIN** (scripts: `--confirm "START AGAIN"`), then backs the database up and checks
  the backup before seeding. A missing database is seeded straight away, as before.

## 2. User guide wording (docs/USER_GUIDE.md)

**Signing in for the first time.** Your administrator gives you your email, employee ID and a one-time setup code
(like `ABCD-EFGH-JKMN`). Type your email and employee ID, then the setup code when asked, then set up two-step
verification. The code works once and for 7 days. No code, or has it run out? Ask an administrator for a new one.

**Adding someone (administrators, leaders, recruiters).** After saving, Sign-in details show their employee ID and
one-time setup code. Send them privately; you will not see the code again. Add people on the office computer or over
https, or the code is not shown (an administrator then gives them one from Team).

**Give a setup code (administrators).** Team → the person → Edit profile → Sign-in setup → Give a setup code. Type
your own two-step code. The new code replaces any older one.

**Resets (administrators).** Reset two-step verification and Reset PIN ask for your own two-step code, sign the person
out everywhere and show the setup code they sign in with next.

**Trial mode (administrators).** The banner stays until the owner goes live. Settings → Trial mode lists the steps.
The one-tap demo accounts only work on the demo company: they cannot change your own people, anyone's real pay,
payments or the office settings ("Demo accounts cannot …"). Use your own administrator account for real work.

**Editing someone (administrators).** Making someone an administrator or stopping them being one, setting an
administrator as left, and changing someone's sign-in email or employee ID ask for your own two-step code.

**Recovery codes on the office Wi-Fi.** If you turn on two-step verification from your phone over the office Wi-Fi,
the recovery codes are not shown, to keep them safe. Make them on the office computer: Settings → Security → New
recovery codes.

**Pay tile.** "Overdue pay" in red means a payday has passed and that pay is still not marked paid. Ask the office.

**Going live (the owner).** Stop Linear Hub, run `npm run golive`, type GO LIVE, start it again and sign in with the
email, employee ID and setup code it printed, then set up two-step verification.

## 3. Commands (README / DEPLOY)

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `npm run golive` | Ends the trial (section 1). Stop Linear Hub first. `-- --confirm "GO LIVE" --owner-email … --owner-first-name … --owner-last-name …` for scripts. |
| `npm run seed` | Builds the demo database. Refuses a live database, one with people of your own, or one in use; asks for START AGAIN (or `-- --confirm "START AGAIN"`) and backs up first. |
| `npm run setup-code -- <email>` | Prints a new one-time setup code for that person (works while Linear Hub runs). Says so when they need none. |
| `npm run 2fa:reset -- <email>` | As before, and prints the setup code they sign in with next. |
| `npm run pin:reset -- <email>` | As before, and prints the setup code their next sign-in needs. |

All work from the top folder of the repository too (`package.json` scripts added at all three levels; the
`linear-micro-crm` `seed` script now passes arguments through).

**Environment**

| Variable | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `LM_TLS_CERT_FILE`, `LM_TLS_KEY_FILE` | Both set: built-in HTTPS on `PORT`. Neither: plain http (the Mac trial, or behind Caddy). One alone: refused at start-up. |
| `LM_DEMO_ACCOUNTS` | `0`: no one-tap demo sign-ins. `1`: show them even online. Unset: on, except on a server with built-in HTTPS or `TRUST_PROXY` (online). Trial mode stays until `npm run golive` either way. |
| `TRUST_PROXY` | Unchanged, but keep `HOST=127.0.0.1` with it (the server warns otherwise). Also turns the one-tap demo sign-ins off by default. |

## 4. API changes (docs/API.md)

- `POST /auth/login` `{ email, employeeId, pin?, setupCode? }`. For an account that has not finished setting up:
  200 `{ requiresSetupCode: true }` (no cookie) until the same body comes with `setupCode`; a wrong, used or expired
  code → 401 `{ error, details: { requiresSetupCode: true, reason: 'wrong' } }`. Order: credentials → PIN → setup
  code → two-step challenge. Missing and wrong codes count against the login limiter. Exempt: one-tap demo accounts
  in trial mode. The audit sign-in reason `wrong_setup_code` ("Failed sign-in: wrong, used or expired setup code");
  a sign-in with a code reads "… with a setup code".
- `GET /auth/me` and the sign-in answer: an administrator's answer carries `trial: { trialMode, demoSignIns }` while
  in trial mode.
- `GET /auth/demo-accounts`: empty unless the database is in trial mode and the settings allow the one-tap sign-ins
  (by default not with built-in HTTPS or `TRUST_PROXY`; `LM_DEMO_ACCOUNTS=1` forces them on).
- `POST /auth/login/2fa`: after a PIN reset, a right two-step code also uses up the setup code step one accepted; if
  that code was replaced or expired meanwhile → 401 `{ details: { requiresSetupCode: true, reason: 'wrong' } }` (start
  again).
- `POST /users`: `role: 'admin'` needs `code` (the caller's fresh two-step code). 201 `AddPersonResult` adds
  `setupCode` (null over an insecure connection from another device, with `setupCodeWithheld: true`) and
  `setupCodeExpiresAt`; `signInInstructions` include the code.
- `PATCH /users/:id`: `code` is needed for making someone `admin`, changing an admin's `role`, setting an admin's
  `status` to `left`, a different `email`, or a different `employeeId` (case does not count). 400 "Type the 6-digit
  code…" without it.
- `POST /users/:id/setup-code` (new; admin; not your own; not someone who has left → 409; `{ code }`) → 200
  `SetupCodeIssued { userId, setupCode, expiresAt }`. Secure connection only. Audited ("Gave … a new setup code").
- `POST /users/:id/2fa/reset` `{ code }` → 200 `SetupCodeIssued` (was 204).
- `POST /auth/pin/reset/:userId` `{ code }` → 200 `SetupCodeIssued` (was 204); the next sign-in needs the code even
  with two-step on.
- `POST /client-accounts/:id/reset-pin` and `/reset-2fa` `{ code }` → 200 `ClientAccountReset` (the account plus
  `setupCode`, `setupCodeExpiresAt`). `PATCH /client-accounts/:id` with a new `email` needs `code`.
- `POST /campaigns/:id/client-accounts` (invite): `ClientInviteResult` adds `setupCode`, `setupCodeExpiresAt`
  (`setupCodeWithheld`); the instructions include the code.
- `POST /recruitment/candidates/:id/stage` to `hired`: the answer adds `hiredSignIn: HiredSignIn` (name, email,
  employee ID, team, `setupCode`, `signInInstructions`).
- `POST /auth/2fa/enable` over an insecure connection from another device: `{ recoveryCodes: [], recoveryCodesWithheld: true, message }`.
- Demo accounts during the trial: every write route is checked against `server/src/lib/demoWrites.ts`
  (DEMO_WRITE_RULES); one with no rule is refused. `GET /payments/batches/:id/file` and real people's bank details
  are refused too.
- Refusals: demo accounts 403 `{ details: { code: 'demo_account', trialMode: true } }`; insecure connection 403
  `{ details: { code: 'insecure_connection' } }`; step-up without two-step 403
  `{ details: { code: 'two_factor_required', twoFactorRequired: true } }`; missing code 400 "Type the 6-digit code
  from your authenticator app"; wrong code 400; too many wrong codes 429.
- Needs attention: administrators' feed starts with `security:trial:demo-on` / `security:trial:demo-off` (danger,
  href `/settings#trial`, action "How to go live", `at` = now so it sorts first).

## 5. LOGIC changes (docs/LOGIC.md)

- Trial mode: `meta` keys `ext:access:trial-mode` ('1'/'0'), `ext:access:demo-people` (user ids of the demo company),
  `ext:access:seed-marks` (highest id per company table after the seed). The demo seed sets them; the access
  extension sets them once for an older database (on only for a demo database with its demo staff intact); golive
  clears them and `seed_today` (keeps `seeded_at`).
- Restricted demo = trial mode and (a demo person or a one-tap demo account). What it may write:
  `lib/demoWrites.ts` DEMO_WRITE_RULES, one rule per write route ("METHOD /pattern"): allow; people(…) — the people
  it touches are the demo company or the caller; money(…) — only the demo company's pay; record(table) — a record at
  or below the seed mark, or one the audit trail shows a demo account (or the caller) created; deny. Unlisted writes
  are refused; a test checks every route has a rule. Reads are allowed except DEMO_READ_RULES (payment file, real
  people's bank details). Mounted as `demoGuard` in app.ts after `authenticate`, before the write limit and the audit.
- One-tap sign-ins allowed (`lib/demoTeams.ts demoAccountsAllowed`): `LM_DEMO_ACCOUNTS=0` no, `=1` yes, otherwise yes
  unless `TRUST_PROXY` is on or `LM_TLS_CERT_FILE` is set.
- Step-up on staff edits: shared `staffEditStepUp(existing, patch)` (the server and the Edit profile form).
- `needsSetupCode` = not a one-tap demo account and (no two-step, or `users.setup_code_required`). Codes:
  `setup_codes(user_id, code_hash, reason, created_at, created_by, expires_at, used_at, held_by)`; hash =
  SHA-256 of `setup-code:` + the normalised code; issuing deletes the person's unused codes; using one clears
  `setup_code_required`. No two-step: the code is used at `/auth/login`. Two-step on (after a PIN reset): `/auth/login`
  sets `held_by` to the new two-step challenge's token, and `/auth/login/2fa` uses the code held for that challenge
  only after the two-step code is right.
- Golive's keep rules (lib/goLive.ts, lib/demoRecords.ts): seed marks for an upgraded database come from the audit
  trail (the lowest id of an audited create after the seed, minus one) and from when statements arrived; a seeded
  record stays when an audit change row by a person of the office's own names it, when it names one of the office's
  people, or when any staying row references it (PRAGMA foreign_key_list over every table, plus a few unlinked
  columns), repeated until nothing changes.
- Sessions: `sessions.token` holds SHA-256(hex) of the cookie value.
- Secure request = `req.secure` (TLS, or `X-Forwarded-Proto: https` from a trusted proxy) or a loopback peer
  (`req.ip` behind a trusted proxy, else the socket).
- Golive's removal rules are in section 1 (lib/goLive.ts).

## 8. Decisions

- "Promoting to admin/office" is read as the **admin role** (the only role with office-wide rights); the `office`
  position carries no extra rights, so changing a position needs no code.
- The one-tap demo accounts are removed at go-live even if renamed: they were open to anyone on the network.
- A setup code is a secret like a recovery code, so it is withheld over plain http from another device.
- Demo accounts write from an allowlist (`lib/demoWrites.ts`), not a denylist: a route nobody has decided about is
  refused for them, so a new feature cannot open a gap during the trial.
- Accounts of the office's own starter file (`server/data/starter-team.json`) are still one-tap during the trial
  and listed on the sign-in page (that is what the starter team is for). They are restricted demo accounts while
  trial mode lasts (their own gauges and account only), and they stay, as ordinary accounts needing a setup code, at
  go-live. `LM_DEMO_ACCOUNTS=0` turns their one-tap sign-in off too.
- Built-in HTTPS has no certificate reload (a renewal needs a restart) and no http→https redirect; behind Caddy
  (route A) neither matters.
- The pay tile and the dialog/pop-up fix are small changes in the phone package's files (dashboard and components).
