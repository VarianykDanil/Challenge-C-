# Release 1.5: the phone package

What changed for phones, why, and what each document needs to say. Findings fixed: UX-1, UX-2, UX-3, CLM-5,
RELIABILITY-MISSED-1, UX-MISSED-2, UX-MISSED-4, UX-5, UX-6, UX-7, UX-8, UX-10, UX-11, CODE-MISSED-3, SEC-9, the

## Summary

- **Works with no signal, for real.** Once Linear Hub has been opened with signal on a phone (over https, or on the
  server's own computer), the app is saved on the phone: every page opens with no signal, even after the phone has
  closed the tab, after a new version was put live, and while the server behind the proxy is stopped or restarting.
  Gauges and check-ins saved meanwhile send when the connection is back.
- **A weak signal never leaves the app on "Loading…".** Reads give up after 15 seconds (5 while Linear Hub is known to
  be out of reach), and the sign-in check when the app opens after 6, so the app opens as the last person and says it
  cannot reach Linear Hub.
- **Opening with no signal lasts only as long as the sign-in.** The phone opens offline as the last person only until
  their session would have ended anyway, and only for reps and leaders.
- **Home-screen app.** Add to Home Screen gets a proper Linear Hub icon and opens without browser bars.
- **Tables read as cards on a phone.** No sideways scrolling; long lists show 10 cards and **Show all**.
- **Check in is easy to find.** A **Today** card at the top of the rep's and leader's dashboard, a **Check in at …**
  item in Needs attention, and a labelled **Check in** button on Quick gauges.
- **Faster to update**, Inter served by Linear Hub itself (nothing from Google), plain words on the dashboards and
  the Events list, pop-ups that never cover a form, numbers checked as they are typed, Settings sections one tap
  away, and the right owner in the copyright notice.

## For users (wording for USER_GUIDE.md)

### If the signal drops (replaces the section of the same name, first bullets and "Keep the page open")

* **When the phone has no signal** an amber strip appears under the top bar: **You're offline: saves will send when
  you're back. Gauges and check-ins wait on this phone.** If the phone has signal but cannot reach Linear Hub (the
  server is down or restarting, the connection is too weak to get an answer, or you are on a different Wi-Fi from the
  office computer during the trial) it says **Can't reach Linear Hub: saves will send when it's back.** It goes away
  by itself as soon as Linear Hub answers; **Try again** checks straight away.
* **Every page opens with no signal** once Linear Hub has been opened with signal on that phone, even if the phone
  closed the tab, you open it from the Home Screen, or a new version came out since. Pages that need fresh figures
  (pay, the leaderboard) say **You're offline. This needs a connection and loads when you're back.** Logging gauges,
  Quick gauges and checking in work as normal and wait on the phone.
* **Opening the app with no signal** carries on as the person last signed in on that phone, so a rep or leader can
  log today's event and check in straight away. Whatever you save waits on the phone and sends once you are signed in
  with signal. This works until the time you would be signed out anyway: 14 hours after you signed in, or after the
  inactivity time (12 hours by default) since the app last reached Linear Hub. After that it shows the sign-in page,
  which needs signal. Signing out removes it from the phone straight away.
* **On a weak signal** the app waits a few seconds, then opens as above and says **Can't reach Linear Hub**; it keeps
  trying in the background and carries on normally as soon as Linear Hub answers.
* **This needs the secure (https) address.** On a plain http:// address (the office Wi-Fi during the trial) the
  browser cannot keep Linear Hub on the phone: it then works as before, and saves only wait while the page stays open.

### A new version

When a new version of Linear Hub is put live, phones download it in the background and show **A new version of Linear
Hub is ready. Reload when you have saved what you are doing.** with a **Reload** button. Until you reload, the app
keeps working as it was, with or without signal.

### Phone and iPad tips (replaces "Add it to your Home Screen")

* **Add it to your Home Screen.** In Safari, open Linear Hub, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. On Android,
  in Chrome, use the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. You get the Linear Hub icon, and it
  opens full screen like an app, with no signal too. On iPhone the icon has its own sign-in and its own saved
  entries, separate from Safari's, so use one or the other for a whole shift.

### Dashboard (reps and leaders)

* **Today** sits at the top when your team has an event today: the event, its times and place, **Check in** (or
  **Checked in 09:17 · on time**), **Log gauges** and **Quick gauges**.
* Then **Needs attention**, your numbers and pay, the team and the next events. On a phone, **Your trend and
  funnel**, **Your week in review** and **Company** fold away behind one button each; tap to open (the phone
  remembers).
* Plain words: **Your aim: 5 sales. That usually takes about 109 stops, 39 at the table, 22 presentations, 9 closes
  and 6 taps.** (on the dashboard and the Events list; Quick gauges shows the start of it on one line: **Aim 5 sales:
  about 109 stops, 39 at the table, …**) and **Expected: about 20 sales (13 to 27) · 7 booked**.

### Checking in (add to the check-in section)

* **Needs attention** says **Check in at BCN — Golden Square** from an hour before your team's event starts until it
  ends, until you check in (or log the day). Before the start it reads **Starts 09:30 · 5 min grace**; once started,
  **Started 09:30**. Its **Check in** button opens Quick gauges for that event.
* On **Quick gauges** the button at the top says **Check in** on a phone too, then the time you checked in.
  (Replaces "On a phone it is a pin icon".)

### Tables on a phone

On a phone every table shows one card per row: who or what first, the amount or status on the right, then every other
column with its name. **Sort by** at the top sorts the cards. A long list shows its first 10 cards and **Show all**
(the number of rows). Ticking **Select all** in a list shows every card first, so you can see everything you ticked.
Nothing needs a sideways swipe.

### Pop-ups

**Needs attention** pop-ups appear at the top of a phone screen for 5 seconds, two lines at most, and go away as soon
as you open a form or tap into a field; the whole message stays in the bell.

### Numbers as you type

In Log gauges the checks run as you type: type 9 sales with 2 taps and the warning shows straight away. A number
that is too big says **Use a number from 0 to 2000.**

### Finance and Settings

* The owner and office open **Finance** on **Company**; everyone else on **My pay**.
* **Check and accept your invoice** (in Needs attention) now opens that week's payslip, with **Accept invoice** and
  **Something's wrong** at the top.
* **Settings** on a phone starts with **On this page**: tap a section to go to it.

## For the owner (OWNER_NOTES / README)

* **"Works with no signal" needs https.** Browsers only keep a web app on the phone (the service worker) on a secure
  address. On the supported go-live (a small UK/EU server with Caddy giving https, DEPLOY.md route A) every phone gets
  it automatically the first time it opens Linear Hub with signal. On the office Mac over plain http:// during the
  trial it does not, and the app behaves as in 1.4 (saves wait while the page is open).
* **While you update or restart the server** (`npm run latest`, a restart after a crash) Caddy answers "502 Bad
  Gateway" for a moment. Phones that have opened Linear Hub before now show the saved app with **Can't reach Linear
  Hub** instead, keep gauges and check-ins, and send them when it is back.
* **Opening with no signal follows your sign-in rules.** A phone opens offline as the last rep or leader only until
  the hours-from-sign-in limit (14 by default) or the inactivity limit (12 by default) since it last reached Linear
  Hub, whichever comes first; the settings are the ones in **Settings → Security**. Admins and recruiters never get it.
* Nothing to install or switch on. Phones pick up each new release by themselves and ask to reload.
* The invoice and contract wording "Works with no signal" is true as written on the secure (https) address; on plain
  http it is "keeps working through signal drops while open".

### Service worker rules (for LOGIC.md)

* **Never** handled: anything not GET, other origins, `/api` and `/api/*` (including a payslip PDF opened as a page),
  and `/sw.js` itself. So the service worker never stores personal data.
* **Pages** (navigations): the network first, with a 4 second limit. With no signal, no answer in time, or any 5xx
  answer (behind Caddy a stopped or restarting app is a 502/503/504 from the proxy): the saved `index.html`. With
  nothing saved: the network's answer, or with no network a one-line "open it once with signal" page (503).
* **`/assets/*`** and other app files: this release's saved copy, then any other saved copy (the release waiting to
  take over, the previous release), then the network (kept in `lm-runtime-v1`, at most 120 files, never an HTML
  answer to a file request). Lookups ignore `Vary` (the app sends `Vary: Origin, Accept-Encoding`; a module import
  carries an Origin header; a hashed file is the same either way).
* **Install** saves the precache into `lm-shell-<version>`, copying hashed `/assets/` files from earlier caches
  instead of downloading them again. **Activate** moves the old release's own bundles (those the new one lacks) into
  `lm-previous-v1` (one release back only), deletes old `lm-shell-*` caches and takes control. A new release waits
  until the person taps **Reload** (`SKIP_WAITING`).

### "Can't reach Linear Hub" (for LOGIC.md, next to the outbox)

A request counts as not reaching Linear Hub (a `NetworkError`: the offline strip, gauges and check-ins wait in the
outbox, the field kit on a cold start) when fetch throws, when a read gets no first answer in time (15 s; 5 s while
the last request already failed; the sign-in check when the app opens 6 s), or when the answer is a 502, 503 or 504
without Linear Hub's own JSON `{ error }` (the proxy's page). Saves (POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE) have no time limit: a
slow save may still have been made. Gauge days and check-ins sent twice are stored once, so a resend after a proxy
504 is safe. While Linear Hub cannot be reached, the app checks the session again every 15 s and as soon as anything
answers; failed pages load again by themselves.

### API changes (API.md)

* `GET /auth/me`, `POST /auth/login`, `POST /auth/login/2fa`: `session` gains `idleHours` (number, optional: the
  admin's "Sign out after idle" setting). `SessionInfo.idleHours?: number` in shared.
* `GET /me/attention`: new item `events:checkin:<eventId>`, kind `events`, for a rep or leader whose team has an event
  today, from 60 minutes before its start until its end, while they have no check-in and no gauge row for it there.
  Severity `info` before the start ("Starts 09:30 · 5 min grace. Tap Check in when you get there."), `warning` after
  ("Started 09:30. …"). Title "Check in at BCN — Golden Square", `href` `/quick?eventId=<id>`, `actionLabel`

  attendance statement, so the feed stays within its 12-statement budget.
* Attention items `invoices:open:*` and `invoices:resolved:*` `href`: `/finance?tab=me&run=<payroll run id>`.
* Content-Security-Policy: "styles inline; fonts, the service worker and the manifest from this server only

* Static files: `/sw.js` and `/manifest.webmanifest` are `no-cache`; `/sw.js` has `Service-Worker-Allowed: /`; a
  missing `/assets/*` file is a 404.

### Client behaviour changes (API.md client notes)

* `apiRaw`: time limits and the proxy rule above (`ApiInit.timeoutMs` to override); `NetworkError.reason` is
  `unreachable`, `timeout` or `gateway`; `appReachable()`.
* TanStack Query: `networkMode: 'offlineFirst'`; a query does not retry a `NetworkError` that timed out or came from
  the proxy, or any `NetworkError` while the browser is offline; queries that failed that way refetch when Linear Hub
  answers again.
* `AuthProvider`: the first `/auth/me` waits at most 6 s; a `NetworkError` with nobody signed in uses the field kit's
  person; while unreachable it re-checks every 15 s and on any answer; a 401, sign-out and `clearSession()` remove
  the kit. The server still checks every request.
* `OfflineBanner` starts shown when the last request already failed. `useLoggableEvents()` uses the saved events at
  once while Linear Hub is known to be out of reach.
* `DataTable` below 640 px: cards (`phone="scroll"` keeps the table). `Column.card`: `lead`, `title`, `aside`,
  `field` (default), `wide`, `actions`, `hidden`. A clickable card is a list item with a visually hidden
  **Open <title>** button (`aria-current` on the open one; the card shows the focus ring while it has focus); no
  `tabindex` or `aria-selected` on the item. More than 13 cards: the first 10 and **Show all N** (`phoneRows`, default
  10, `null` for all); the open row is never hidden; using a select-all box in the header shows every card first.
* `Toast`: `notice: true` toasts give way to dialogs (`DIALOG_OPEN_EVENT`) and focused fields; on phones they sit at
  the top, last at most 5 s and keep to two lines.
* `CheckInButton` `compact` (Quick gauges): always labelled "Check in", then the time. `variant="hero"` for Today.
* `EventPlanStrip`: `row` and `upcoming` read "Your aim: … That usually takes about …"; `quick` reads "Aim 5 sales:
  about …" on one line.
* `Stepper`, `KpiTile`, `FunnelChart`, Finance `?run=`, `copyText()`: as before in this package.
