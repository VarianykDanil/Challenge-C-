# Release 1.5: the phone package

What changed for phones, why, and what each document needs to say. Findings fixed: UX-1, UX-2, UX-3, CLM-5,
RELIABILITY-MISSED-1, UX-MISSED-2, UX-MISSED-3, UX-MISSED-4, UX-5, UX-6, UX-7, UX-8, UX-10, UX-11, CODE-MISSED-3,
SEC-9, the ownership notice (part of CODE-2). UX-4 is not done here (see the end).

## Summary

- **Works with no signal, for real.** Once Linear Hub has been opened with signal on a phone (over https, or on the
  server's own computer), the app is saved on the phone: every page opens with no signal, even after the phone has
  closed the tab, and gauges and check-ins saved meanwhile send when the connection is back.
- **Home-screen app.** Add to Home Screen gets a proper Linear Hub icon and opens without browser bars.
- **Tables read as cards on a phone.** No sideways scrolling for pay, gauges, the leaderboard or any other table.
- **Today's event first.** Reps and leaders see a **Today** card at the top of the dashboard with **Check in**,
  **Log gauges** and **Quick gauges**.
- **Faster first load**, Inter served by Linear Hub itself (nothing from Google), plain words on the dashboards,
  pop-ups that never cover a form, numbers checked as they are typed, Settings sections one tap away, and the right
  owner in the copyright notice.

## For users (wording for USER_GUIDE.md)

### If the signal drops (replaces the section of the same name, first bullets and "Keep the page open")

* **When the phone has no signal** an amber strip appears under the top bar: **You're offline: saves will send when
  you're back. Gauges and check-ins wait on this phone.** If the phone has signal but cannot reach Linear Hub (the
  server is down, or you are on a different Wi-Fi from the office computer during the trial) it says **Can't reach
  Linear Hub: saves will send when it's back.** It goes away by itself; **Try again** checks straight away.
* **Every page opens with no signal** once Linear Hub has been opened with signal on that phone, even if the phone
  closed the tab or you open it from the Home Screen. Pages that need fresh figures (pay, the leaderboard) say
  **You're offline. This needs a connection and loads when you're back.** Logging gauges, Quick gauges and checking in
  work as normal and wait on the phone.
* **Opening the app with no signal** carries on as the person last signed in on that phone, so you can log today's
  event straight away. Whatever you save waits on the phone and sends once you are signed in with signal. Signing out
  removes this from the phone.
* **This needs the secure (https) address.** On a plain http:// address (the office Wi-Fi during the trial) the
  browser cannot keep Linear Hub on the phone: it then works as before, and saves only wait while the page stays open.

### A new version

When a new version of Linear Hub is put live, phones download it in the background and show **A new version of Linear
Hub is ready. Reload when you have saved what you are doing.** with a **Reload** button. Until you reload, the app
keeps working as it was.

### Phone and iPad tips (replaces "Add it to your Home Screen")

* **Add it to your Home Screen.** In Safari, open Linear Hub, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. On Android,
  in Chrome, use the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. You get the Linear Hub icon, and it
  opens full screen like an app, with no signal too. On iPhone the icon has its own sign-in and its own saved
  entries, separate from Safari's, so use one or the other for a whole shift.

### Dashboard (reps and leaders)

* **Today** sits at the top when your team has an event today: the event, its times and place, **Check in** (or
  **Checked in 09:17 · on time**), **Log gauges** and **Quick gauges**.
* Then **Needs attention**, your numbers and pay, the team and the next events. On a phone, **Your trend and
  funnel**, **Your week in review** and **Company** fold away behind one button each; tap to open (the phone
  remembers).
* Plain words: **Your aim: 5 sales. That usually takes about 109 stops, 39 at the table, 22 presentations, 9 closes
  and 6 taps.** and **Expected: about 20 sales (13 to 27) · 7 booked**.

### Tables on a phone

On a phone every table shows one card per row: who or what first, the amount or status on the right, then every other
column with its name. **Sort by** at the top sorts the cards. Nothing needs a sideways swipe.

### Pop-ups

**Needs attention** pop-ups appear at the top of a phone screen and go away as soon as you open a form or tap into a
field; they stay in the bell.

### Numbers as you type

In Log gauges the checks run as you type: type 9 sales with 2 taps and the warning shows straight away. A number
that is too big says **Use a number from 0 to 2000.**

### Finance and Settings

* The owner and office open **Finance** on **Company**; everyone else on **My pay**.
* **Check and accept your invoice** (in Needs attention) now opens that week's payslip, with **Accept invoice** and
  **Something's wrong** at the top.
* **Settings** on a phone starts with **On this page**: tap a section to go to it.

### Troubleshooting table (replaces the amber strip row)

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An amber strip: **You're offline: saves will send when you're back** | The phone has no signal. | Keep logging; it sends by itself. |
| An amber strip: **Can't reach Linear Hub** | The phone has signal but Linear Hub does not answer. | Keep logging; tell the office if it lasts. |
| A blue strip: **A new version of Linear Hub is ready** | A new version was put live. | Tap **Reload** when you have saved. |

## For the owner (OWNER_NOTES / README)

* **"Works with no signal" needs https.** Browsers only keep a web app on the phone (the service worker) on a secure
  address. On the supported go-live (a small UK/EU server with Caddy giving https, DEPLOY.md route A) every phone gets
  it automatically the first time it opens Linear Hub with signal. On the office Mac over plain http:// during the
  trial it does not, and the app behaves as in 1.4 (saves wait while the page is open).
* Nothing to install or switch on. Phones pick up each new release by themselves and ask to reload.
* The invoice and contract wording "Works with no signal" is now true as written on https; on http it is still
  "keeps working through signal drops while open".

### Service worker rules (for LOGIC.md)

* **Never** handled: anything not GET, other origins, `/api` and `/api/*` (including a payslip PDF opened as a page),
  and `/sw.js` itself. So the service worker never stores personal data; the only personal data on the phone is what
  the outbox kept before (gauges and check-ins waiting) plus the field kit's name, role and team.
* **Pages** (navigations): the network first, with a 4 second limit; then the saved `index.html`; with nothing saved,
  a one-line "open it once with signal" page (503).
* **`/assets/*`** and other app files: the saved copy first, then the network (kept in `lm-runtime-v1`, at most 120
  files).
* **Install** saves the precache into `lm-shell-<version>`, copying hashed `/assets/` files from the previous
  release's cache instead of downloading them again. **Activate** deletes other `lm-shell-*` caches and takes control.
  A new release waits until the person taps **Reload** (`SKIP_WAITING`).

### Server

* `server/src/app.ts` (CSP lines owned by this package): `font-src 'self'` (Google Fonts removed from `style-src` and
  `font-src`), new `worker-src 'self'` and `manifest-src 'self'`.
* Static serving: `sw.js` and `manifest.webmanifest` are sent `Cache-Control: no-cache` (like `index.html`), `sw.js`
  with `Service-Worker-Allowed: /`.
* Bug fix: the page fallback (any non-file, non-API path) named `index.html` by its full path, and Express refuses a
  path with a dot-folder in it, so an install under e.g. `~/.local/…` (or a git worktree) answered 404 for `/login`,
  `/finance` and every page. It now uses `root`.
* `server/src/lib/attention/invoices.ts`: the person's "Check and accept your invoice" item links to
  `/finance?tab=me&run=<payroll run id>` ("Open the invoice"); the office's reply links to the same
  ("Open the payslip").

### Client behaviour changes (API.md client notes)

* TanStack Query: `networkMode: 'offlineFirst'`, and a query does not retry a `NetworkError` while the browser is
  offline, so pages say "You're offline" instead of loading forever. `ErrorState` shows the calm offline message for
  a `NetworkError`.
* `AuthProvider`: a `NetworkError` from `/auth/me` with nobody signed in uses the field kit's person; a 401, sign-out
  and `clearSession()` remove the kit. The server still checks every request; nothing is sent until that person is
  signed in.
* `useLoggableEvents()`: with no live list and no cached one, falls back to the field kit's events; keeps the kit
  updated.
* `DataTable`: below 640 px rows render as cards (`phone="scroll"` keeps the table). `Column.card`: `lead`, `title`,
  `aside`, `field` (default), `wide`, `actions`, `hidden`; the first plain column is the title; keys `select` and
  `actions` default to `lead` and `actions`.
* `Toast`: `notice: true` toasts (Needs attention) are removed when a dialog opens (`DIALOG_OPEN_EVENT`, fired by
  `Modal`) or a form field outside the toasts gets the focus. On phones the toast stack sits at the top.
* `Stepper`: valid keystrokes reach `onChange` immediately; out-of-range input shows "Use a number from 0 to N." and
  is clamped on blur.
* `KpiTile`: a size container; the figure uses container units and `white-space: nowrap`.
* `FunnelChart` (narrow legend): full step names ("Stop → table") instead of "S→T"; no text under 11 px.
* Finance: `/finance?run=<id>` opens that payslip on My pay; with no `tab` the owner opens on Company.
* `CheckInButton` `variant="hero"`; `EventForecastChip` `plain`; `EventPlanStrip` `upcoming` reads as a sentence.
* `ClientAccountsCard` Copy uses `copyText()` (falls back to a hidden text box on plain http).

React, React DOM and the scheduler, the router, and TanStack Query build into `react-*.js`, `router-*.js` and
`query-*.js` (vite `manualChunks`): they keep their names between releases, so an update downloads only the app code
that changed (and the service worker copies unchanged files from its old cache).
