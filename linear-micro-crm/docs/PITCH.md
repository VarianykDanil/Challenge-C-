# Linear Hub — the case for Linear MCR

*For the owner. Ten minutes to read. Every number that is an estimate says so, and says how we arrived at it. Where
something has not been tried with a real bank, a real client statement or a real payment provider yet, this page says
that too.*

---

## In one paragraph

Linear MCR already runs on numbers: six gauges per rep per event, judged against the law of averages, and a pay rule
that turns those gauges into a weekly wage. What the business has not had is one place where those numbers live. They
are collected on paper and in WhatsApp, tallied by hand, and answered from memory. Linear Hub is that place: gauges,
events, people, recruitment, pay and paying people, on the phone in everyone's pocket, at an address of Linear MCR's
own. Nothing about how Linear sells changes; what changes is how long it takes to know what happened, and how sure
everyone can be that pay is right.

## Where the time goes today

| Question | How it is answered now | What it costs |
|---|---|---|
| "How did the team do yesterday?" | Each rep messages six numbers to the leader; the leader retypes and adds them up. | Leader time every evening; mistakes in copying; no history anyone can search. |
| "Which part of Jordan's pitch needs work?" | Gut feel, or working out ratios by hand at the end of the week. | Coaching arrives late and is general rather than specific. |
| "How many of last month's applicants reached observation day? Where did the good ones come from?" | Nobody knows without reading back through notes. | Recruitment spend goes where it always has, not where the hires come from. |
| "What am I being paid this week, and when?" | Ask the leader, who asks the office. | Interruptions for the office; uncertainty for the rep; late payments noticed late. |
| "Did the client cancel any of my sales?" | The office reads each client's statement and works it out by hand. | Hours a week, and corrections that are hard to explain. |
| "Was Amelia's team on time this month?" | Nobody is recording it. | Punctuality cannot be coached or rewarded because it cannot be seen. |

## What Linear Hub does

**For a rep.** Sign in on your phone, or open the Linear Hub icon on your home screen. Your dashboard shows today's
event first, this week's stops, sales and conversion, your rank in your team, your pay so far and your next payday.
Check in at the venue with one tap. Log your gauges as the day goes with **Quick gauges**, or after the event: the form
will not let you enter more presentations than people at the table. If the signal drops, your saves wait on the phone
and send themselves when it comes back. Your ratios are coloured against the company benchmarks, with a sentence saying
which stage to work on. Your pay shows how it was worked out (base or commission, whichever is higher), and each week
you accept your self-billing invoice or say what is wrong with it. You add your own bank details; only the last digits
are ever shown. The Learning page holds the COD pack lessons and the campaign pitches.

**For a leader.** Schedule your team's events, book who is working, and see a plan for each person at each venue.
Log a day for anyone on your team. See the team funnel, every member's ratios side by side, and who moved on the
board. Confirm the days that need a leader's word before they are paid. Sign off lessons and teach-backs. You can read
across the company; you can change only your own team's things.

**For the recruiter.** A dashboard that leads with the pipeline: open candidates, this week's interviews, overdue
follow-ups and hires. Interview schedules and interview sheets. Move someone to **Hired** and their account is created
on the spot, with a one-time setup code to hand them.

**For the office.** Campaigns with what each client pays and what the rep earns. Weekly pay worked out when a week
closes, with cancellations taken from the clients' statements (read from Gmail). Self-billing invoices, VAT where a
person is VAT registered, and a payroll spreadsheet for the accountant. Pay people from Linear Hub with a bank payment
file or a direct connection, with a fresh code for every payment and a second administrator's approval. Expenses, revenue and margin by week and
by campaign. An audit trail of every change, every sign-in and failed attempt, and every export.

**For the charities and clients.** A login of their own to a page for their campaign: they post updates and ask for
rate changes, and see nothing about reps' pay or personal details.

## What you get, and what it costs

- **A licensed system with a monthly service.** Noryn System Ltd built Linear Hub, owns it and supports it; Linear MCR
  uses it under the written agreement between the two companies: a one-off price for the build and a monthly service
  fee, both set out in the agreement. The monthly service covers fixing faults, updates, keeping the bank, payment and
  Gmail connections working as those services change, and looking after the server and its backups with you.
- **Your own small server, in Linear MCR's name.** Linear Hub runs on one small Linear MCR-owned server in the UK or
  EU, at an address of your own (for example `hub.linear-mcr.co.uk`), with HTTPS. Running costs go straight to the
  providers *(October 2026 prices, check before buying)*: the server about €5–10 a month, the domain about £10 a year,
  off-site backup storage about €4 a month. There are no per-user fees.
- **One sign-in on every phone, anywhere.** Reps at a venue in Bolton or Liverpool use it on 4G exactly as in the
  office. Added to the home screen it opens like an app, and once a phone has opened it, it opens again without
  signal and keeps saves until the signal is back.
- **Backups that leave the building.** Every night the server makes a checked backup and an encrypted copy of it that
  goes to separate storage. The key that locks bank details and two-step sign-in is printed once on a recovery sheet
  that you lock away. Before go-live, a backup is restored and checked, so we know the way back works.
- **The office Mac stays for the trial only.** During the trial week Linear Hub runs on the office Mac over its
  Wi-Fi or hotspot. That is fine for trying it; it is not how it should run for real (it stops when the Mac sleeps or
  leaves the office, and phones elsewhere cannot reach it).

## The outside services it can connect to

Every connection is optional and switched on by the office. Nothing else is sent anywhere: no analytics, no
advertising trackers.

| Service | What for | What it sends | Where it stands |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Your bank, by payment file** (Starling, Revolut Business, or a general Faster Payments file) | Paying a week's runs | Nothing by itself: Linear Hub makes the file, you upload it and approve it in your bank | Layouts follow the banks' published guides. Start with the **1p test file** to check your bank takes it. |
| **Revolut Business, Wise Business or TrueLayer (Open Banking)** | Paying approved runs directly | The payee's name, account details, amount and reference, for each payment you approve | Built to each provider's published API and tested against local stand-ins. The first real check is a sandbox payment with your own account, which Settings → Payments walks you through before anything goes live. With Wise, Linear Hub creates the transfers and you fund them in Wise. |
| **Gmail** (read-only, an app password) | Reading the clients' commission statements | Nothing: it only reads emails from the senders you list, and never sends, moves or deletes mail | Built on sample statements. The first statement from each client waits for you to check its columns once. |
| **YouTube, Vimeo or Google Drive** | Learning videos you choose to link | What any embedded video sends; YouTube plays from youtube-nocookie.com until someone presses play | Optional; uploads to Linear Hub itself work too. |
| **Let's Encrypt** (through Caddy on the server) | The HTTPS certificate | The domain name | Renewed automatically. |
| **Off-site storage** (a Hetzner Storage Box or any S3-compatible bucket) | The nightly encrypted backup copies | Encrypted files the provider cannot read | Set up by the installer at go-live. |
| **An uptime monitor** (optional, e.g. UptimeRobot) | A text or email if the site stops answering | Nothing: it only asks `https://<your address>/api/health` whether it is up | Free plans are enough. |

## Security, in plain words

- **Signing in.** A work email and employee ID, a one-time setup code from the office the first time, then a 6-digit
  code from an authenticator app, for everyone, charity contacts included. The office can also require a PIN. Everyone
  is signed out 14 hours after signing in (the office can choose 4 to 24 hours), or after 12 hours unused, and at once
  when they are marked as having left.
- **Who sees what.** A rep changes only their own things and sees only their own pay. A leader changes only their own
  team's things. Employee IDs, bank details and private notes are shown only to the people who need them.
- **Money.** Bank details, payment keys and tokens are encrypted on the server and never shown in full. Nobody can
  confirm their own change of bank details: a person's own change waits for an administrator, and details an
  administrator types wait for the person (or a second administrator). Every payment needs a fresh two-step code;
  once two administrators use two-step sign-in, a second one must approve it too; and a week is never paid twice.
  Payment connections start in test mode and go live only when you type PAY FOR REAL.
- **On the internet.** The server answers only over HTTPS, its firewall lets in nothing else, and security updates
  install themselves every night.
- **The record.** The audit trail records every change, every sign-in and failed attempt, and every export and
  download. It does not record page views.
- **During the trial.** The demo company and its one-tap demo accounts can never touch real bank details or
  payments, and `npm run golive` removes them for good on go-live day.

## Rollout plan

**Trial week — on the office Mac.** The demo company shows every screen; the starter team tries their own accounts.
Nothing real is paid.

**Go-live day — on the server (about half a day with Noryn).** Buy the server and the domain in Linear MCR's name, run
the installer, move the trial database across, go live, print the recovery sheet, test a restore. The steps are in
[`DEPLOY.md`](DEPLOY.md).

**Weeks 1–2 — pilot one team.** One leader and their team (six to eight people) use Linear Hub for every event,
alongside the WhatsApp routine so nothing is lost. We watch three things: does every rep log every event day, does
the leader stop retyping, does the pay match. Payments: a sandbox payment first, then one person, then the team.

**End of week 2 — review.** Did logging stick? Did the leader's evenings get shorter? Did anything in the numbers or
the pay surprise us? Fix what needs fixing.

**Weeks 3–4 — all teams.** Team by team, a short walkthrough for each leader. Recruitment moves onto the board the
same fortnight.

**Week 6 — retire the old routine.** WhatsApp stays for conversation; gauges, pay and candidates live in Linear Hub.

## Questions we expect

**Is the data secure?** See *Security, in plain words* above. In short: HTTPS, two-step sign-in for everyone, a fresh
code and a second approver for every payment, encrypted bank details, a firewall, nightly security updates and an
audit trail.

**Does it work on phones, away from the office?** Yes: that is what the server is for. It is built for a rep standing
in a car park: large tap targets, a dark theme, tables that read as cards on a small screen, and saves that wait when
the signal drops.

**Can we get the data out?** Every main table exports to a spreadsheet or PDF, and there is a payroll spreadsheet for
the accountant. The database is a standard SQLite file you own.

**What if someone logs the wrong numbers?** The form refuses impossible funnels and dates. A rep corrects their own
entry; a leader corrects their team's; the office corrects any. A day that looks like it should not be paid waits for
a leader to confirm it. Every change is in the audit trail.

**What happens when someone leaves?** Mark them as left: they are signed out everywhere at once and cannot sign in
again; their history stays so the numbers still add up.

**Who owns what?** Linear MCR owns its data, on a server in its own name. Noryn System Ltd owns the software and
licenses it to Linear MCR (see [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE) and the agreement).

**What if Noryn could not support it any more?** Linear Hub is written in common, widely known tools (TypeScript,
React, Node.js, SQLite), and [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md) explains to another developer how it fits together,
where the rules live and how to change it safely.

**Will it need Supabase or a bigger database?** Not for Linear MCR's size: one small server handles hundreds of
requests a second. A bigger database is a later option only if Linear opens several offices or hundreds of people use
it at once ([`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md) says why and what it would take).

**How long does it take to learn?** A rep: under five minutes. A leader: half an hour. The recruiter: half an hour.
The office: an hour or two, most of it on pay and payments.
